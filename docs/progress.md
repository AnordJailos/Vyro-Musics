## Suggested next steps

1. Run ./setup.sh on your machine and report what flutter analyze and flutter test say. Until then, every Built item is unconfirmed.
2. Connect an email provider and an SMS provider, and add your Google and Apple client IDs. Without them, real people cannot sign up with those methods.
3. Send listening history from the app, so the statistics pages fill with real numbers.
4. Synced lyrics and offline downloads, or the Studio uploads and the streaming pipeline.
